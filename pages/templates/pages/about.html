About Prime Crop Care

We are a team of agronomists, tech enthusiasts, and supply-chain experts dedicated to empowering farmers with data-driven solutions and high-quality inputs.

Our Story

Founded in 2020, Prime Crop Care was born out of a passion for sustainable agriculture. We saw that smallholder farmers often lack access to reliable soil information and quality inputs, leading to poor yields and wasted resources.

Today, we bridge that gap by combining cutting-edge soil scanning technology with a trusted supply chain for agro inputs. Our mission is to make precision farming accessible to every farmer, from small plots to large estates.
